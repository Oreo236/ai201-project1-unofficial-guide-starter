Safe Living Environments

Cornell has partnered with the Building Division of the City of Ithaca to create an online system that displays the relative safety information of City rental properties, including: fire safety, notifications systems, emergency egresses, construction type and property use.
Safety Program

This
online safety tool
draws from data collected by the Building Division through inspections of rental properties and recognizes landlords and properties that have met the necessary requirements to provide a safer residential environment. It also provides our students and their parents an opportunity to make more informed decisions in choosing safe living environments.
The following building and Certificate of Compliance related elements have been identified as critical items for use in determining a safety score:
Fire Protection (sprinkler systems)
Fire Extinguishers
Notification Systems (alarm systems including smoke and carbon monoxide detectors)
Construction Type
Applicable Code
Property Use
Data is updated daily to provide accurate descriptions of the safety of each property listed in the online tool. Cornell and the City are working together to include additional safety elements including fire separations and emergency egress. Properties outside of the city limits are currently exempt.
