Living Off Campus

Cornell Housing supports students, staff, faculty, and affiliates living off campus by offering guidance, education, and referral services to help you make informed housing decisions, to help you prepare, and identify your needs to guide decision making.

Qualified Off-Campus Housing
Cornell has a 2-year
residential requirement
for full-time, Ithaca-based campus, first-year and second-year undergraduate students, including a required meal plan. In the second year of residency, students may live in residence halls, and are permitted to live in approved, university-affiliated housing.
Please note: Cornell University and Off-Campus Housing do not make endorsements or recommendations of any property owner, landlords, or housing units. Students are encouraged to do their own research and reach out to these organizations directly for specific details about housing availability, costs, and community expectations.
