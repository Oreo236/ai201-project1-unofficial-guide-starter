Original Post (Ok-Negotiation-8499):
I got into an MS program at Cornell and have a budget of about $800/month. I've been really considering housing at Auden Ithaca, but I've seen mixed (mainly bad) reviews. Is it as bad as people say? Also, if you know any alternatives in the same price range, I'd appreciate your advice.

Reply (Studentofcornell):
Lease signing: Sign a lease with someone you know. I got randomly placed with 2 other apartment-mates and it was horrible. We clashed on how the apartment should be kept.

Contacting staff: Calling or walking in is always better than email. I tried email and never heard back.

Room sizing: Rooms are a little small but I was paying $750 and it wasn't terrible.

Parking: Only $40/month, plenty of spots, but the lines in the parking lot are very faded.

Laundry: The laundry room closed down for a month or 2. They extended the shuttle service to a laundromat further down the hill. Laundry requires a money card that has to be loaded.

Shuttle: Pretty consistent on arrival/departure times. There's an app to track the bus.

Location: About a 15-minute walk uphill to the center of campus. 30-minute walk to Stewart Park. Closer to downtown and the Walmart area.

Reply (anonymous):
I stay in Auden, it's really not that bad. Great roommates and staff is very responsive. The only downside is laundry machines sometimes break and take about a month to get fixed. The shuttle service is also not reliable so you might need an e-bike. I'd give Auden a solid 7/10.
