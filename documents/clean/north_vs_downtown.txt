Thread: Housing: northeast Ithaca vs downtown?
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/Cornell/comments/1evn3cn

Original Post (gjnk97):
I am a foreign exchange student visiting Ithaca this fall, working at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. I can't decide where to live and won't have a car. Northeast seems good as I'd be a short walk from work, but I'm worried I'd miss out on the city and social life. If I stay downtown, I'd commute 40 minutes taking 2 different buses. How isolating would it be living far from downtown?

Reply (RelevantShock):
Look around University Park or Gaslight Village apartments. Use the savings on rent to get a bike. It's about 15 minutes (flat ride) to the Lab of Ornithology from there, and a short walk to Ithaca Bakery or Ithaca Coffee Company in Triphammer Mall. Not too hard to get to Tops for groceries. The 30 or 32 bus stops aren't far — you can take them to campus for grad happy hours at Big Red Barn, or all the way downtown for restaurants.

Reply (mhaithaca):
Agree with the suggestion of apartment complexes behind Triphammer Marketplace, which is walking distance to lots of shopping. That's just one bus route away from the Lab of Ornithology, or reasonable biking distance in good weather. Same is true of apartments on Pleasant Grove Road near Corners Community Shopping Center — quick bus trip to work, very easy to get elsewhere in town.

Reply (ithacaster):
Cornell students don't generally go downtown for social reasons. IC students do. Cornell students stay more in Collegetown when they go out at night. It depends on what you want for a social life.
